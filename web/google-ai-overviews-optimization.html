Google AI Overviews: What Official Documentation Says

Google AI Overviews build on Search's core ranking and quality systems. Google says there are no additional requirements or AI-specific optimizations; eligibility starts with an indexed page that is eligible to appear with a snippet.

How Google AI Overviews Select Citation Sources

Google's generative search features do not operate as an isolated crawler. According to Google Search Central, AI Overviews represent an inference layer evaluated over Google's standard web index:

  1. Candidate Selection from the Search Index: Pages must be crawled by Googlebot, indexed, and eligible to be shown in Google Search with a snippet. Google's core ranking and quality systems determine which pages are retrieved; Google has not published an AI-specific candidate threshold.
  2. Query Fan-Out and Retrieval: Google documents query fan-out and retrieval-augmented generation. The exact model and passage-selection details can vary between AI Overviews and AI Mode.
  3. Snippet Directive Evaluation: Directives such as nosnippet, max-snippet, or data-nosnippet limit what text Google may show from a page in Search and its AI features. They affect display controls, not a separate legal-permission test.
  4. Supporting Source Links: AI Overviews and AI Mode may show a wider, more diverse set of supporting links. Google does not guarantee that a page will be cited even when it meets the requirements.

Measure the result: Use the Generative AI performance report in Search Console to see how content performs in Google's generative AI features. No third-party tool has access to Google's internal ranking or AI systems.

Googlebot vs. Google-Extended: Clarifying the Crawl Boundary

Googlebot controls crawling for Google Search. Google-Extended is a separate robots.txt token for whether crawled content may be used for training future Gemini models and for grounding in Gemini Apps and Grounding with Google Search on Vertex AI. It does not affect a site's inclusion in Google Search and is not a ranking signal.

Crawler User-Agent Token Primary Purpose Impact on Google Search & AI Overviews
Googlebot Crawls and renders pages for Google Search indexing. Required for Search eligibility. Blocking Googlebot can remove your site from Search and its AI features.
Google-Extended Controls use of crawled content for training future Gemini models and for grounding in Gemini Apps and Grounding with Google Search on Vertex AI. No effect on Search inclusion or ranking. It also does not determine AI Overviews eligibility. It can limit use in other Gemini grounding surfaces.

Controlling Extraction with Standard HTML Snippet Directives

Google provides standard controls that limit what text can be shown without blocking indexing:

  • <meta name="robots" content="max-snippet:150">: Limits textual snippet extraction to a maximum character count.
  • <span data-nosnippet>Proprietary data</span>: Excludes the enclosed text from snippets and previews in Google Search, including its AI features.
  • <meta name="robots" content="nosnippet">: Prevents any snippet or generative overview display in search results.
