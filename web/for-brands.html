Brand team questions

What does CiteAura measure for a brand?

Public-site crawlability, extractable facts, structured data, citation signals, and labeled AI answers for a defined question and provider cohort.

Can a brand verify a change after deployment?

Yes. Re-crawl the site and compare a defined before-and-after period while preserving the sampling mode and cohort.

Does a higher score guarantee an AI mention?

No. The score summarizes checks in a defined scope. Provider retrieval, model updates, and answer variance remain separate evidence.
