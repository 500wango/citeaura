What is a GEO diagnostic report? Evidence-backed action plan

A GEO diagnostic report is an evidence-backed audit that analyzes AI engine citations, identifies crawl and content gaps, and delivers prioritized engineering tickets. This sample demonstrates how CiteAura turns a public-site crawl and a labeled AI baseline into actionable fixes, acceptance checks, and a client-ready delivery pack.

Example only: all domain names, prompts, rates, and ticket outcomes on this page are illustrative. A real workspace reports the sampled engines, sampling mode, raw answer replay, and evidence coverage that were actually measured.

What evidence is included in a GEO diagnostic scope?

A GEO diagnostic scope is the structured boundary separating technical crawlability from generative answer sampling. The report keeps technical diagnosis separate from visibility measurement, so a customer can verify what was checked and what remains unmeasured across public pages, AI answers, machine discovery files, and competitor prompts.

Why are competitors cited while brand domains are omitted?

Generative engines omit brand domains when pricing, technical specs, or FAQs are unextractable from the DOM or blocked by robots.txt. Verbatim ground-truth traces from AI engine responses show exactly why competitors are cited while brand pages are omitted.

What engineering tickets close verified citation gaps?

A GEO action ticket is an engineering task containing concrete acceptance criteria and automated regression checkers. Every recommendation has an owner, an evidence link, and a checker that can reopen the ticket after a regression.

How are client deliverables and verification gates validated?

Delivery and verification is the formal validation process confirming that recommended site fixes were deployed and re-sampled before client handoff. The final pack contains an audit report, execution plan, ticket log, acceptance checklist, draft risks, and build map. The pack is marked diagnostic-ready until implementation evidence is complete.
