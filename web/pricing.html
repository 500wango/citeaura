Yes. Eligible new workspaces receive a 14-day Starter trial with no credit card requirement.

Are API model costs included?

CiteAura is BYOK-first. You configure the providers you intend to use, and the workspace shows which providers are measured, funded, or unmeasured.

Does a plan guarantee AI mentions or rankings?

No. Plans provide the workflow and evidence needed to diagnose and verify work. Provider behavior remains outside a plan guarantee.
