CiteAura Research & Engineering Group

CiteAura Research Team

Search Systems Architects · Evaluation Engineers · Standards Contributors

In strict alignment with Google Search Central's E-E-A-T quality and transparency standards, all technical guides, benchmarks, and diagnostic protocols published on CiteAura are engineered and vetted by the CiteAura Research Team—the multidisciplinary working group behind the platform's open-source GEO engine.

Rather than relying on unverified speculative claims, our publications are derived from deterministic software execution, large-scale multi-model prompt sampling, and direct analysis of search engine and AI crawler specifications.

Core Research & Engineering Disciplines

Systems & Retrieval

Search Systems & RAG Architecture

Investigates how LLM search engines (Perplexity Sonar, Google Gemini, OpenAI Search) retrieve, rank, and cite web documents. Focuses on vector chunk extractability and context-window answerability.

Protocols & Crawlers

Crawler Standards & RFC 9309

Analyzes robots.txt longest-match precedence rules, edge cache invalidation, machine discovery via llms.txt, and HTTP user-agent negotiation across OpenAI, Anthropic, and Perplexity crawlers.

Data Science & QA

LLM Sampling & Evaluation

Develops reproducible prompt replay protocols, statistical variance controls, and deterministic mention-rate scoring across diverse model families and multilingual regional cohorts.

Semantic Web

Entity Extraction & Schema Validation

Specializes in Schema.org JSON-LD graph construction, brand fact library synchronization, and structured entity disambiguation to prevent model hallucinations.

Our Engineering Principles

  • Open-Source Engine Foundation: Our diagnostic framework is powered by an auditable Python engine with over 240+ deterministic unit tests, ensuring all findings are reproducible.
  • Explicit Provenance: Every mention metric distinguishes between Parametric Model Knowledge, Live Grounded Web Retrieval, and Manual Product Surface sampling.
  • No Speculative Ranking Guarantees: We report verifiable technical facts, crawl accessibility, and empirical citation presence—never fabricated ranking promises.
