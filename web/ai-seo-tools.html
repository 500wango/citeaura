What is AI SEO?

AI Search Engine Optimization (AI SEO) or Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the technical and content engineering practice of making your brand visible, verifiable, and citable by Large Language Models (LLMs) and AI-driven search engines.

Unlike traditional SEO, which focuses on keyword density, backlinks, and ten blue links, AI SEO centers on semantic understanding, factual extraction, and entity recognition. The goal is no longer just driving clicks to a landing page, but ensuring your brand is accurately recommended when a user asks an AI agent a complex, multi-layered question.

The Shift to Generative Search

With tools like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity shifting user behavior from "searching" to "asking," brands must ensure their information is structured in a way that models can retrieve and synthesize it confidently. Missing this shift means your brand might disappear from the conversation entirely, even if you rank #1 in traditional search.

Successful AI SEO relies on technical AI visibility audits to ensure crawlers can access your pages and correctly interpret the facts associated with your brand.

Why Traditional SEO Tools Fall Short

Most SEO software was built for the era of Google's ten blue links. They measure rank tracking, keyword search volume, and domain authority. However, these metrics do not apply directly to AI search engines.

  • No "Ranking" Position: LLMs generate synthesized answers, not a static list of links. Traditional rank trackers cannot measure narrative inclusion.
  • Black-Box Crawling: AI engines use different crawlers, user-agents, and retrieval mechanisms. A page that Googlebot can read might be blocked or misunderstood by ChatGPT-User.
  • Entity Over Keywords: AI answers rely on entity relationships and knowledge graphs. Standard on-page SEO tools miss the nuances of how a model maps your brand to a category.

This is where purpose-built AI SEO tools like CiteAura step in to bridge the gap.
