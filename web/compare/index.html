Evaluation Standards & Testing Methodology

In accordance with Google's quality guidelines on first-hand evaluations and transparent assessments, our comparisons adhere to rigorous criteria:

  • Separation of Concerns: We differentiate between prompt listening (scraping public chat interfaces) and technical extractability auditing (evaluating robots.txt, semantic DOM structure, JSON-LD schema, and server response headers).
  • Provenance of Evidence: We separate API parametric knowledge, web-grounded RAG retrieval, and manual product surface observations.
  • Balanced Assessment: We clearly highlight the specific enterprise use cases where competitor architectures are preferable (e.g., broad social listening vs. deterministic engineering remediations).
  • Continuous Updates: Competitor product capabilities are audited on a quarterly basis against public documentation and empirical testing cohorts.
