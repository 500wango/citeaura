Frequently asked questions

Why does Schema.org markup matter for AI search?

Answer engines like Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Google Gemini ingest structured JSON-LD data to connect brand entities, product capabilities, official pricing, and author expertise into factual knowledge graphs.

Which Schema types are most important for SaaS and brands?

Organization (official company identity and logo), SoftwareApplication or Product (features and offers), FAQPage (direct question-answer extraction), and Article (editorial authority and dateModified).

Can Schema markup compensate for missing text on the page?

No. Google and AI search engines enforce that structured data must match visible on-page content. Hidden or unsupported markup can result in manual penalties or extraction disregard.
