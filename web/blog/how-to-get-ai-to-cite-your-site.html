How to Write Pages That AI Cites (Answerability)

Search Engine Optimization rewarded comprehensive, long-form guides stuffed with keyword variations. Generative Engine Optimization rewards something entirely different: Answerability. When an AI search model evaluates your page, it looks for compact, self-contained semantic units that resolve specific questions with high factual confidence.

Updated 1 September 2026 · 10 min read · Content Engineering

By Marcus Vance

The Mechanics of Passage Answerability

Retrieval-Augmented Generation models do not read web pages like human browsing visitors. They execute a multi-stage chunking and extraction pipeline:

  • Passage Tokenization: The document is split into chunks of approximately 200–500 tokens.
  • Dense Embedding Search: Vector embeddings score the semantic similarity between the user's prompt and each passage chunk.
  • Reranking & Extraction: The reranker scores chunks for directness, penalizing passages that rely on pronoun references ("It allows them to...") or bury the conclusion after 300 words of background.

The Inverted Pyramid Answer Pattern

To maximize the probability that an AI model selects your passage for synthesis, structure every major section using the inverted pyramid pattern:

1. Lead with the Core Assertion (First 30–40 Words)

State the direct definition or answer immediately under the heading using clear subject-predicate syntax. Avoid warm-up phrases like "In order to understand this concept, we must first look at...".

2. Provide Quantitative or Structural Evidence

Support the assertion with concrete data, specific parameters, native HTML tables, or explicit constraints.

3. Add Caveats or Boundaries

State limitations or prerequisites cleanly at the end of the passage.

Before & After Copy Transformations

Example 1: Product Definition

  • ❌ Vague Marketing Copy (Fails Extraction):
    "At AcmeCorp, we believe in unlocking the future of marketing. Our next-generation platform empowers growth leaders with cutting-edge artificial intelligence to revolutionize their digital presence."
  • ✅ High-Answerability Copy (Earns Citation):
    "AcmeCorp is an automated B2B customer data platform that unifies product telemetry, billing events, and CRM records into a central PostgreSQL data warehouse."

The 5 Golden Rules of GEO Copywriting

  1. Name the subject explicitly: Replace ambiguous pronouns (it, they, our platform) with your actual brand or entity name in key definition sentences.
  2. Use semantic markup: Enclose key concepts in <dl>, <dt>, and <dd> tags rather than unstyled text blocks.
  3. Avoid verdict stacking: Do not end every paragraph with generic slogans or repetitive conclusion sentences.
  4. Publish native tables: Render comparative matrices in semantic HTML rather than images or JavaScript canvas widgets.
  5. Audit with real prompts: Test your rewritten pages across high-intent queries using CiteAura's AI visibility audit.
