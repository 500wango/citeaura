Measure and diagnose AI brand visibility

AI brand visibility (Generative Engine Optimization) is the measurable presence, citation frequency, and factual accuracy of a brand across AI search engines. These articles answer specific questions about ChatGPT mentions, crawler blocks, llms.txt, and client diagnostic packs. Product setup lives in Docs. CiteAura is the workspace that records the evidence.

What are the core AI visibility investigation tracks?

The core AI visibility investigation tracks are crawler access verification, content answerability structuring, Schema.org knowledge graph anchoring, and empirical mention sampling. To maximize citation readiness across ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI Overviews, our research team organizes audit workflows into four systematic phases:

  1. AI Crawler Access: Verify robots.txt and WAF rules to ensure fetchers like GPTBot, Claude-SearchBot, and PerplexityBot are unblocked.
  2. Content Answerability & Extractability: Structure key brand facts into semantic tables, modular lists, and answer-first paragraphs.
  3. Structured Entity Markup: Deploy Schema.org JSON-LD with valid sameAs links to anchor brand identity in knowledge graphs.
  4. Empirical Sampling & Verification: Sample fixed prompt cohorts across parameterized and web-grounded modes to track real mention rates.

  2. OpenAI Crawlers & GPTBot Documentation — OpenAI Platform Technical Specifications (2024–2026).
  3. Google Search Central: Creating Helpful, Reliable, People-First Content — Google Search Central (2024–2026).
  4. RFC 9309: Robots Exclusion Protocol — M. Koster, G. Illyes, H. Zeller, Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF, 2022).
  5. Schema.org Technical Specifications — W3C Schema.org Community Group.
