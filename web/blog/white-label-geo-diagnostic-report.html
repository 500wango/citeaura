White-Label GEO Diagnostic Reports for Agencies

A white-label GEO diagnostic report is a client-ready pack that shows what the site blocks, what sampled models said, and which tickets to assign—without the agency's measurement tool branded on the cover. It is a point-in-time artifact, not a promise that ChatGPT will mention the client. CiteAura compiles the current audit, ticket log, and verification evidence into a downloadable pack with optional agency branding.

Updated 20 August 2026 · 6 min read

By CiteAura Editorial Team · Evidence and documentation

What the report should contain

  • Site findings — crawlability, extractable HTML, heading structure, and structured data on the live domain.
  • Labeled samples — question, raw answer, date, and sampling mode: API · Model knowledge, API · Web-grounded retrieval, or Manual · Product surface.
  • Action tickets — owner, target page, requested change, and acceptance check. Skip generic SEO checklists that are not tied to this site.
  • Readiness states — diagnosis, visibility, and implementation are separate. A finished audit is not a finished asset pack.

Clients can act on a ticket. They cannot act on a slide that only says “improve AI visibility.”

What must stay out of a client pack

  • Unlabeled screenshots from one ChatGPT session presented as a baseline.
  • Assets still marked for factual review, including draft /llms.txt or JSON-LD that was not checked.
  • Guaranteed mention, rank, or citation language. That claim is false and it will not survive a re-sample.
  • Internal tool chrome if the engagement is white-label. Agency name and confidentiality notice belong on the cover; the measurement vendor does not.

Rebuild the pack after the site or the question set changes. An older ZIP is a snapshot, not the current workspace.

How CiteAura builds the pack

Set the agency header under Delivery → White-Label Branding, then build a new pack. The job zips the current audit, action matrix, ticket log, verification evidence, and implementation assets. Review readiness before you send it: diagnostic files can be client-ready while generated pages still need an editor.

Setup is in Verify & Deliver. Measurement method is in How to Measure ChatGPT Brand Mentions.

How to present it without overclaiming

Lead with what to change on the site. Put mention rates next to sample count and mode. If a provider was not measured, say unmeasured. A later sample after tickets ship is a new cohort, not a warranty.

The pack does not guarantee that any model will mention, cite, or rank the client. It gives the agency a repeatable evidence trail.

FAQ: white-label GEO reports

What belongs in a white-label GEO diagnostic report?

Include site findings, labeled samples, action tickets, verification evidence, and clear readiness states for the audited client.

Can a GEO diagnostic report guarantee AI mentions?

No. It records a dated evidence trail and the actions to take; it does not guarantee a future mention, citation, or ranking.

When should an agency rebuild a GEO diagnostic pack?

Rebuild it after the site, question set, audit, or verification evidence changes so the pack remains a current snapshot.
