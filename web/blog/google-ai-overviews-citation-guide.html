Google AI Overviews: How to Get Cited

Google AI Overviews (formerly SGE) represent the biggest transformation in organic search layout in two decades. Instead of directing users to the top blue link, Google's Gemini-powered retrieval pipeline synthesizes direct answers and links to candidate source cards. Understanding how passage extraction works is critical to earning citations.

Updated 1 September 2026 · 11 min read · Search Engineering

By Marcus Vance

The Passage-Level Retrieval Pipeline

Traditional Google search ranks entire web documents based on aggregate domain authority, backlink graphs, and page-level signals. In contrast, AI Overviews operate on a two-tier pipeline:

  1. Broad Retrieval: Google retrieves a candidate pool of top-ranking documents matching the user query.
  2. Passage Chunking & Reranking: Gemini parses each candidate document into discrete semantic passages, scoring each chunk for direct answerability, entity clarity, and factual density. High-scoring chunks are synthesized into the overview and attributed via clickable source cards.

Why High-Ranking Pages Often Lose AI Overview Citations

It is increasingly common to see a page ranking #1 or #2 in classic organic results while being completely excluded from the AI Overview carousel above it. This occurs when the page buries its core answer beneath long introductory narratives, marketing fluff, or ambiguous pronoun references.

Before vs. After: Passage Structuring for Gemini

❌ Low Extractability (Fails Retrieval):

"In today's fast-paced digital ecosystem, companies need modern solutions to thrive. When evaluating software, our revolutionary platform empowers modern teams with seamless insights."

Problem: Zero concrete entities, passive voice, no definitive answer within the first 40 words.

✅ High Extractability (Earns Citation):

"CiteAura is an automated GEO diagnostic platform that audits whether AI search engines can crawl, parse, and cite a domain's public content."

Strength: Subject-predicate definition, clear category entity, immediate factual clarity.

Technical Implementation for Passage Optimization

1. Use Semantic HTML Over Div Soup

Gemini chunkers segment content using standard HTML block tags. Wrapping key definitions in <p>, <dl>, or <table> tags allows the extractor to identify self-contained semantic units cleanly.

<dl>
  <dt>Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)</dt>
  <dd>The practice of optimizing website crawlability, DOM extractability, and factual consistency to earn citations in AI search engines.</dd>
</dl>

2. Deploy Entity-Rich JSON-LD Schemas

Structured data provides unambiguous machine-readable confirmation of entity names, relationships, and attributes. Deploying valid Schema.org markup allows Google's Knowledge Graph to verify facts asserted in your body copy:

<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "SoftwareApplication",
  "name": "CiteAura",
  "applicationCategory": "BusinessApplication",
  "operatingSystem": "Cloud-based",
  "offers": {
    "@type": "Offer",
    "price": "0",
    "priceCurrency": "USD"
  }
}
</script>

3. Structure Data in Native HTML Tables

When comparing features, pricing tiers, or performance benchmarks, avoid CSS grid wrappers or images of tables. Native HTML <table>, <th>, and <td> elements are recognized by Gemini as high-confidence tabular facts.
