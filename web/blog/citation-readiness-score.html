Citation Readiness Score: 5 Dimensions

Before you can fix an AI visibility gap, you need a deterministic way to measure whether your website meets the technical standards required for LLM citation. CiteAura's Citation Readiness Score breaks down site evaluation across five quantitative dimensions.

Updated 1 September 2026 · 11 min read · Technical Framework

By Marcus Vance

The 5 dimensions at a glance:

  • D1: Crawl Accessibility (25%): HTTP status, robots.txt allow rules, and WAF clearance for AI crawlers.
  • D2: DOM Extractability (25%): SSR HTML density, clean text extraction ratio, and heading hierarchy.
  • D3: Structured Entities (20%): JSON-LD schema validity (Organization, Product, FAQPage) and entity disambiguation.
  • D4: Content Answerability (15%): Inverted pyramid definitions, tabular facts, and unambiguous entity references.
  • D5: Authority & Consensual Links (15%): DR ≥ 30 directory presence, root /llms.txt manifest, and citation references.

1. The 5-Dimension Scoring Matrix

CiteAura calculates a composite readiness score out of 100 based on the following diagnostic checks:

2. Dimension 1: Crawl Accessibility

Crawl accessibility evaluates whether AI fetchers can retrieve your pages without receiving 403 Forbidden, 503 Service Unavailable, or Cloudflare challenge pages. If this dimension scores below 80%, all downstream visibility is blocked.

3. Dimension 2: DOM Extractability

Extractability measures what percentage of your core text survives headless HTML-to-Markdown parsing. It flags pages where critical information is trapped behind client-side React mounts, nested unsemantic divs, or modal overlays.

4. Dimension 3: Structured Entities & Schema

Structured entities provide explicit machine-readable facts (pricing, founder, compliance certifications) that LLMs use to verify claims. This dimension validates syntax and cross-references assertions against public copy.

5. Dimension 4: Content Answerability

Answerability audits how well your copy resolves specific search questions within the first 40 words of each section, penalizing introductory fluff and pronoun ambiguity.

6. Dimension 5: Authority & Knowledge Signals

This dimension checks for a live root llms.txt file and presence across authoritative third-party directories (DR ≥ 30) that corroborate your brand identity.

7. Automated ticket generation from failing checks

CiteAura does not leave you with a passive scorecard. Any check scoring below the pass threshold triggers an automated remediation ticket in your workspace backlog, ready to assign to engineering or content teams.

Frequently asked questions

How often should citation readiness be re-calculated?

CiteAura recalculates readiness automatically after every deployment or on a scheduled weekly cadence to catch regression drift.

What is considered a good Citation Readiness Score?

A score above 85/100 indicates high readiness for AI citations. Scores below 60/100 typically indicate severe crawl blocks or JavaScript hydration extraction failures.

Can an agency white-label the Citation Readiness report?

Yes. Agencies can export the complete 5-dimension report with custom branding, radar charts, and ticket backlogs as part of client delivery packs.
