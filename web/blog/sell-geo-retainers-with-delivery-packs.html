Positioning GEO to Existing SEO Clients

When presenting GEO to existing clients, highlight the shift in buyer search behavior:

  • Show their current citation gap: Run a baseline CiteAura audit demonstrating how ChatGPT recommends competitors for their primary category queries.
  • Highlight zero-click loss: Explain why traditional keyword impressions are dropping as users consume direct answers in-engine.
  • Provide a concrete engineering roadmap: Present GEO not as experimental magic, but as technical crawlability, structured schema, and verifiable content answerability.
