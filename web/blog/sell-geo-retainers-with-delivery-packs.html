For Agencies: Structure & Sell GEO Retainers With Delivery Packs

As clients ask why their brand is omitted from ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude search results, digital agencies have an opportunity to package Generative Engine Optimization into high-margin recurring retainers. Here is a practical framework for tiering services, pricing deliverables, and generating client delivery packs.

Updated 2 September 2026 · 11 min read · Agency Playbook

By Marcus Vance

A Practical Packaging & Pricing Model for GEO Retainers

Agencies can structure GEO retainers around a tiered packaging model anchored on prompt cohort depth and technical ticket execution:

Retainer Tier Suggested Pricing Model Prompt Cohort Scope Monthly Deliverables Ideal Client Profile
GEO Foundation $2,500 – $3,500 / mo 20 buyer prompts across 3 engines Monthly white-label diagnostic report + 4 technical tickets Mid-market B2B SaaS & e-commerce
GEO Growth $5,000 – $6,500 / mo 50 prompts across 5 engines Bi-weekly sampling + 8 technical/copy tickets + llms.txt linting Scaling growth-stage tech companies
Enterprise Authority $8,000 – $10,000+ / mo 100+ multi-region prompts + competitor benchmarks Weekly sampling + unlimited tickets + directory submission support Multi-product enterprise brands

Positioning GEO to Existing SEO Clients

When introducing GEO to existing clients, focus on observable search behavior shifts:

  • Highlight the Citation Gap: Run a baseline CiteAura audit showing whether generative AI search results cite their primary domain or third-party review aggregators.
  • Address Changing Search Habits: Explain how users increasingly consume synthesized answers directly inside AI search interfaces.
  • Deliver Deterministic Workflows: Position GEO as rigorous technical crawlability, structured entity definitions, and passage answerability rather than unverified speculation.
