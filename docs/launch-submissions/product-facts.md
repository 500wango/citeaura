## Short description

Evidence-based GEO software that measures AI brand visibility, audits public websites, turns gaps into engineering tickets, and verifies the fixes.

## One-line value proposition

Find out why AI engines do not cite your brand, then turn the evidence into fixes your team can verify.

## Long description

CiteAura is an evidence-based Generative Engine Optimization platform for brands and agencies. It crawls and audits a public website, measures brand visibility across configured AI engines, separates model-knowledge signals from web-grounded and manual product-surface observations, and turns findings into structured engineering tickets. Teams can implement the work, re-crawl and re-sample, verify acceptance criteria, and export a client-ready delivery pack.

CiteAura is BYOK-first: teams connect the model providers they intend to use and keep inference costs visible. It does not promise guaranteed AI rankings or mentions. Every measured result is labeled by sampling mode, source, model/version where available, and timestamp.
